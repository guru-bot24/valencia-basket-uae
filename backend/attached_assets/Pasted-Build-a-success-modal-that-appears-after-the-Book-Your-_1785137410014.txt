Build a success modal that appears after the "Book Your Free Trial" form is submitted successfully. It should feel premium, minimal, and match the look below. It must look and behave well on both desktop and mobile.

Trigger & overlay

Show the modal only after a successful form submission; hide/blur the form behind it.
Full-screen fixed overlay backdrop: rgba(0,0,0,0.4), centers the card both axes, fade-in transition (~200ms).
Card scales/fades in gently (e.g. from scale(0.96) + opacity 0 to full).

Interaction

Closeable via: the × button, "Back to site", the Escape key, and clicking the backdrop (but not clicks inside the card).
Subtle hover states: primary button darkens slightly (~
#20BD5A), secondary button border/text brighten, × button background lightens.
Trap focus within the modal while open and return focus to the page on close; the modal should be keyboard-accessible and screen-reader friendly (appropriate role="dialog", aria-modal="true", and an accessible label).

Responsive

Desktop: centered card as described.
Mobile: card should sit comfortably with ~16px side gutters, near-full width, slightly reduced padding and heading size (as noted). Buttons stay full width and finger-friendly (min ~44px tall). Ensure the whole modal fits without awkward scrolling on small screens; if content is tall, allow the card to scroll internally rather than the page.

Styling notes

Use the site's existing font family throughout.
Colours: dark 
#16130F, white and translucent-white accents, brand orange 
#E8722C used only for the top bar and the three step icons, WhatsApp green 
#25D366 for the primary button only.
Keep it flat and clean — no gradients, no heavy shadows beyond the card's drop shadow.
